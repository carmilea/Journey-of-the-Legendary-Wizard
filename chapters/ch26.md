"Come in." A voice came from inside.

Winnie gave Maureen a small smile then pushed the door open.

It was a laboratory. Racks and racks of glassware sat upon a long stone bench. Lamplight reflected off the polished brass of various instruments. An old man dressed in white and gold robes was standing with his back towards the door. He was facing the bench, staring intently at a test tube that was boiling with black flame. He wore glasses, and his grey hair was neatly combed.

[Warning: high-energy radiation source detected.]

What reagent is that? Tally asked.

Later.

You always say that.

I know.

A strong chemical smell stung Maureen's nose. The black flame lapped against the glass, but emitted no heat whatsoever.

The old man turned away from the bench. "Ah, Winnie, good afternoon."

Then he looked at Maureen. "And you must be Maureen Olsen."

Maureen bowed her head wordlessly. The bow went a beat deeper than she intended.

"Yes, this is Maureen Olsen," Winnie said.

"I see." Adrian nodded. "Hegel has apprised me of the situation through a messaging spell. It is within my powers, under an old agreement that I made with the Dark Forest long ago, to accept you, child. But, before we begin, I must ask you again. Are you willing to become my apprentice?"

"It will be my honour." Maureen answered quietly.

Adrian's gaze softened at that.

That's a change, Tally noted.

Is that a good thing? Maureen thought back silently.

Unknown. No data available regarding kind wizards. Please ask again in one year.

"Very well." The old wizard smiled. "As you may know, it is traditional for a mentor to provide their new apprentice with some sort of gift on our first meeting. Ah, there we are." He reached up and adjusted his glasses, pushing them back into place on the bridge of his nose. "Maureen... Look into my eyes."

Maureen looked up into Adrian's eyes, and her vision blanked.

The colour began to drain out from the room, sucked into some unknown void of dead silence and darkness. There was nothing in Maureen's display, just a blank black screen and not even Tally's voice to keep her company.

Darkness poured out from Adrian's white and gold robes, his lips moved though no sound came out. His eyes were like the sun, bright and blinding, and when they opened wide golden fire burst forth and consumed Maureen. Pain flooded through every part of her being and her senses blurred. Maureen tried to scream, but she had no voice.

"Maureen?" Winnie tapped her on the shoulder. "Maureen?" Her voice sounded distant and close at the same time.

Maureen jerked her head up and found herself staring at the unchanging world around her. Adrian was still standing in front of her while Winnie was by her side.

"What?" Maureen's throat was as dry as smoke. Cold sweat ran down her back and her legs felt like jelly. Tally? Where did you go?

[Exposed: unknown force field, unknown energy radiation. Spirit power: abnormal. Blood flow 110%. Adrenaline: rising. Platelets: abnormal.] A series of body diagrams appeared in her vision, with important organs marked in red. [Body quality: improving.]

"Just checking," Winnie said, "Are you alright?"

"Yes." Maureen lied.

The world around her slowly came into focus again.

Racks of glassware stood on a long stone bench. A pungent smell assaulted her nostrils and made her eyes water. It was the smell of chemicals, something she was very familiar with. A test tube was boiling under a black flame that emitted no heat, and the lamplight glimmered off the polished brass instruments.

Lost you for a moment there, Tally noted, its voice warm and quiet once more.

I lost myself, Maureen responded silently, her lips never moving.

[Gold fire / Sunset Canyon fire. Same: no data. Colour: different.]

Maureen's hands were cold. Adrian had taken away her will with one sentence, and he didn't even need to lift a finger to do it.

The gold in Adrian's eyes faded away, and he stared at her.

"A Middle Knight." His voice remained calm and quiet. "I never expected your body to be this strong."

Months of hiding, and it took him only one look to see through you, Tally said.

A mentor was always going to see through my camouflage.

Then the mentor must be the only one who can see through it, Tally noted.

Winnie turned to Adrian and frowned gently. "You shouldn't have sprung it on her like that."

Adrian smiled apologetically, then took out a crystal ball and handed it to Maureen. "The Basic Meditation Method, apprentice level. You can use it now. Go and rest, come back tomorrow morning."

He looked around the lab. "Where is Lilith? She should be escorting you."

Winnie sighed. "At a party again." She put a hand on Maureen's shoulder. "I'll take care of it."

Adrian nodded in agreement. "Thank you, Winnie."

The old wizard bowed his head, and Maureen and Winnie returned the gesture before they walked out of the laboratory together.

[Lilith: absent. Habit: parties.]

* * *

Winnie had probably wanted to chat along the way, but after taking one look at Maureen's exhausted face she said little, and quietly led her to the dormitory district.

The corridors were black stone, cold underfoot, and their footsteps echoed between the rows of identical doors, with their brass locks glinting dully against the dark stone walls. Tally kept count of the number of turns they made since leaving the lab, murmuring them under its breath so that it could lead Maureen back, should the need arise. She thanked it silently for its thoughtfulness.

"Maureen," Winnie said as she stopped at a door. "Rest well. And don't go wandering about, understand?" Then she turned and walked away slowly.

Maureen knew that Winnie meant it kindly, but in the world of wizards and magic-users, wandering into areas where one shouldn't be could have any number of unpleasant results.

She took out a dull brass key and turned it in the lock. The mechanism inside clicked heavily before it disengaged, allowing the door to swing open. As it did so, lights came on automatically within the room.

Tally admitted that it had no data on the automatic lights and was offended by this omission. Be offended quietly, please, Maureen told the chip silently.

The rooms were small, but clean and sensibly laid out. There was a bedroom, living room, washroom, and even a study. Maureen shut the door behind her, then walked across to the bed and threw herself onto it. She slept like a dropped stone until the evening.

* * *

Maureen woke to the sound of running water, then sank into the deep bathtub as hot water poured into it. She let out a long breath and relaxed, letting the heat penetrate her muscles. She raised her arms and stretched, feeling the strength surging back into them, feeling her muscles filling out again. Full and solid.

For a few minutes she just luxuriated in the sensations, allowing herself to enjoy the simple power of her own body now it was healed at last.

She flicked her fingers, indicating to Tally that she wanted an analysis of her body. Tally showed [Strength +0.4, Agility +0.3, Constitution +0.3 → 7.9 / 8.0 / 8.1]. These are the hardest gains I have logged at the top of a band, Tally informed her smugly.

I can believe it, Maureen agreed silently. It's taken me forever to get this far and Adrian managed it in one meeting.

Now she was up around the peak of a Middle Knight's body, maybe with a little more she could break into the Upper Knights. At that thought she smiled.

How much do I need? Maureen asked silently.

Tally was silent for a long moment. I do not have exact data for the Upper Knight line so I will not guess. Maureen sighed. As the water cooled, she got out, dried herself off and dressed in fresh clothes before slipping the Seal back into its inner pocket.

* * *

Maureen sat at the desk and looked down at Adrian's crystal ball concentrating all her attention on it. The sphere was cloudy, the lamplight reflecting off the surface sliding around it as the sphere sat there cool and smooth under her hand. Then the ball started to push into her. Words and images flooded her mind blinding her sight. Her head began swelling with the pressure of the information. She clutched at her temples trying to stop her head from bursting.

[Massive information intake. Cerebral blood supply insufficient.]

Tally warned her.

[Shall I catch the deluge and sort it first?]

Maureen mentally said yes and the stages ticked across her display.

[Collecting…]

[Filtering redundant…]

[Identifying junk…]

[Deleting.]

Maureen's head began to feel light and clear again.

That is much better, she thought, than trying to sort through it myself.

Tally responded, I've been waiting since the treasury to hear you say that.

Let's see if I have this right, she thought. A seed provides an outline of the Spirit Runes based on effort and aptitude. But to incorporate them into a Rune Array a meditation method is required. Each method gives the completed array different properties.

The first twelve Spirit Runes only require simple meditation to construct so Scarlette taught them as basics. Without a method though no one can become a Wizard Apprentice. With Tally she was starting to build up a picture. The array was the water and the methods were how you processed it.

Ink or tea she suggested.

Tea, it replied.

So the first twelve are just vapour condensing. Same for everyone no matter what method is used later.

The lamp burned steadily lighting the plain wooden desk. Outside the dormitory study there was silence, the black-stone walls and doors keeping out even the faintest noise. All that could be heard in the room was her slow breathing as she concentrated on the task before her.

[Basic Meditation Method: parsed. Transfer?] Tally asked.

Transfer, Maureen thought silently and the meditation method rose to the surface of her mind becoming clearer with each second.
