They would kill it while it slept. Whispers passed between them and plans were made. Before the beast could fully awake spells would flash and sizzle bringing its life to an abrupt end. Aaron stood near the cavern's northern wall, Cross Sword at the ready just in case.

The serpent's musk filled the air clogging Maureen's nostrils with the scent of old prey. Ground bones formed a gritty layer on the cavern floor that shifted beneath her boots. Smoke from the torches pooled under the rock roof.

A huge coil of blackness lay at the cavern's centre, rising and falling with each slow regular breath. The beast's scales glistened like wet slate in the torch light. A whispering chant rose from Jay, Wolf and Sim, "Pansisomen…" and "Ailerista…". Acid green dripped from Jay's fingertips, black smoke curled from Wolf's hands, while deep red fire crackled and danced in Maureen's palm.

Tally highlighted the serpent's skull with a targeting reticule and displayed a casting counter above it. *Keep that small,* Maureen thought silently at Tally. It obeyed, shrinking the numbers till they were almost invisible. *I can't make Sim any smaller,* it told her.

The fire in Maureen's palm was beginning to sting. She could feel the heat through her spell and a bead of sweat trickled down from her hairline. She held her breath.

"Ha! We've nearly done it!" Wolf laughed out loud.

> Eyes open.

Tally flashed at her a heartbeat before Maureen saw them herself.

The serpent opened its eyes. The earth-yellow irises had vertical slit pupils like a cat's. Anthony shouted, "Attack! Now!"

The serpent blurred forward. Sim screamed as he was snatched up by the lower body, his spell disintegrating in a shower of sparks. He pounded at the serpent's snout with both fists, screaming for help. The serpent ignored him, tilting its head back until Sim disappeared into its mouth, swallowing the boy alive.

Maureen watched the bulge move down the serpent's throat, even as Sim continued to shout for help. Then the bulge flattened out, and Maureen felt her stomach drop, her body suddenly cold.

*Breathe. Three long. One short,* Tally told her in its warm voice. She did so silently, willing the fireball in her hand to stay intact.

* * *

Maureen let fly the fireball from her hand, aiming it at the serpent's throat. The creature rolled once more on the floor, shrieking with rage. But when it reared again, only a few scales had cracked, and she could see nothing more than a couple of bloody streaks across the coal-black flesh.

> Scales cracked: 3. Wound closing.

> Mana 5 → 3.5

> Scent change noted. Sulphur? Never noted this from snakes. Source?

Anthony shouted, "It has strong physical defence and strong spell resistance! This is some kind of mutant!" The black serpent charged them, and Anthony yelled, "Fight together or none of us get out of here!"

There was a rush of air past her face as the coil whipped by. The grit slid beneath her heels as she adjusted her stance. The torches guttered as the wind from the serpent's coils blew through the cavern.

> Movement. Coil left. Faster than anything on file.

*Where is its weak point?* Maureen asked Tally silently.

> No data available for this animal. Based on library plates, throat appears to be least armoured.

> Tail lashing every two heartbeats. One. Two.

> Now. Wall. Kick.

Maureen flipped backwards, kicked off the rock wall, and dragged her Cross Sword down the serpent's body. A long, deep cut opened up in the coal-black flesh. Just then, a pale-white Undead Wind Arrow punched through the creature's body, leaving a bloody hole behind it.

Hot blood sprayed across her sleeve. The scent of iron mixed with the sulphurous smell of the serpent. The force of the Cross Sword's cut ran up Maureen's arms, into her shoulders.

> Regeneration: visible. ~1 finger-width per breath.

Maureen's lips never parted. The only response she gave to Tally was one wordless profanity, spoken loud and clear in her head.

*I am merely the messenger,* Tally said.

> Tail — Aaron —

Tally's warning came too late. The tail had already slammed into Aaron, sending him flying like an empty sack. He spat out a mouthful of blood as he was thrown from sight. "Aaron!" Maureen shouted.

Her throat burned from the force of her shout, but she didn't care. Dust still drifted lazily through the air where Aaron had disappeared.

*Where?*

> By sight: behind rockfall at north wall.

*Lives?*

*No data.*

Tally pinned Aaron's last known bearing into the edge of her display without being asked. She could see the bearing of Aaron's last location pinned with that single letter: N.

"Listen up, people. This isn't an ordinary black snake, it's some mutant type or something. Maybe even a subspecies of some higher energy creature." Anthony shouted over the coils of the serpent. "Damn it, we were tricked!"

Maureen kept silent, waiting for Anthony to give orders.

"Olsen, Jay, Wolf. Draw its attacks while I do something about it."

Maureen nodded as she gripped her Cross Sword tightly. Her lungs burned from the exertion, her arms ached from the repeated blows. Her eyes stung from the sweat that poured down her face.

Maureen jumped into the hollow of the rock wall and cast Hand of Negative Energy, a Rank Zero spell. A shadowy hand formed in the air and crashed down upon the serpent, crushing its neck. The hand's touch ate away scales and flesh alike in grey-green, rotten-egg corrosion.

[Mana 2.0]

*I know,* Maureen said silently.

*You keep reaching for a third spell with your left hand.*

*Oh.*

Maureen glanced at her left hand and moved it back to her hilt.

"Anthony! Are you ready?" Maureen shouted across the cavern.

Anthony opened his eyes and held out his hands, which were shaking so much that the antique lacquered box he held almost slipped from his grip. He began to chant something that sounded like a hymn, if hymns had darker lyrics than most people would approve of.

Black smoke poured out of the box and flowed across the distance toward the serpent. Like wet wool, the black smoke wrapped itself around the serpent but could not be shaken off. The serpent twisted its neck this way and that, but the smoke clung on tenaciously. It even seemed to seep underneath the serpent's scales.

The coldness of Anthony's spell reached Maureen's teeth from across the cavern. The stench of rotten eggs filled the air.

Her particle sense detected a wave of dense negative energy rolling through the cavern.

[Energy Density: Negative] [Mana: ?]

Tally created a new bar graph, which was shooting off its own scale because this was more dense than Gandolf's attack ever was.

Maureen frowned in concentration.

*Frown later. Dodge now.*

* * *

"Attack!" Anthony said, his face pale from using the box.

Acid Missiles, Undead Wind Arrows, and fire poured into the struggling serpent. Maureen hurled her final Blast Fireball into the serpent's thick neck. [Mana 0.5] Maureen's mana bar was nearly empty.

Maureen drew a throw-line back to the serpent's eye, and waited for the head to rise again.

> On the rise. Now.

The serpent's head snapped up in one last spasm, and Maureen threw her Cross Sword into its eye. The serpent's head exploded in a crossfire of elemental energies. The force of the blast threw Maureen's sword deep into the rock wall beside the serpent. Hot blood rained down on Maureen, filling the air with the stink of sulphur. Small pieces of the serpent's scales clattered against the stone floor around Maureen's boots.

*Your sword is in the wall. You are nearly in the wall,* Tally said.

Maureen didn't respond, she was still standing after all.

She relaxed her shoulders and let out a breath. "It's over."

For half a second, Maureen's hands hung empty in the air, her fingers loose and relaxed.

The alert from Tally came through sound, not sight. [Warning: incantation behind you, by sound. Simons-Sado. Jay's voice.]

*Too late to dodge? The Seal?*

Maureen's right hand snapped to her inner pocket, closing on the smooth surface of the Seal. "Vonmenjaye Soss Orenmosa!" she said as the thorn armour formed around her body, just as Jay's fireball crashed against her back.

[Seal 0.0 … 1.8 s]

The explosion threw Maureen forward into the rock wall. A cloud of grey dust billowed into the cavern.

Maureen felt the hard thorns of her armour dig into her skin. The smell of sap and iron filled her nose, as the force of the blow thundered through the vines. Grit filled her mouth as the red light faded in the grey dust.

*Release now. No one can see,* Tally's voice said in her head.

The voice was low and quick.

Maureen released the armour after two seconds.

[Damage: Bruising. Left shoulder. Seal glow: Dimming.]

*How much is left in it?*

*No data.*

Maureen slipped the Seal back into her inner pocket.

Jay's voice came through the dust. "Finally! I can kill her now. I was waiting for that damned serpent to die first, but now I can finally kill her."

He still seemed to be angry at her for killing the venom lizard with an arrow in front of everyone, and angry at being billed five stones for the antidote.

"I'm of the Olivia Family, and she dared treat me like that!"

Anthony stared at Jay, pale from using the box, stunned at the rage in the flaxen-haired man's face.

"That's right, Young Master Jay," Wolf said, small eyes blinking rapidly. "She deserves to die for sure."

Something settled inside Maureen's chest. Her hands were steady, her face gave away nothing.

Her hair was black, but a layer of dust coated it. The robe had a tear on the left shoulder where she had hit the rock wall. Her tongue felt the gritty texture of stone dust.

Maureen stepped out of the dust cloud. Her robe was torn, but she looked unhurt, only a little dishevelled. "It seems like you two want me dead." She spoke aloud.

> Jay: Low on mana

> Wolf: Hurt

> Anthony: Still, by the torches.

*Leaving?* Tally's voice asked in her head.

*Settling accounts,* Maureen said silently.

*Oh, excellent! Then I should open up a column for this,* Tally said, sounding pleased with itself.

Maureen turned to look at Jay and Wolf. "Since I'm not dead, how about we settle our accounts properly?" She felt the corner of her mouth lift.

*Logged as a smile,* Tally said.

As Jay and Wolf stared back at her, Maureen reached over her shoulder and pulled the Cross Sword slowly out from the rock wall. The steel screeched against the stone.
