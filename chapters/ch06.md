Dawn Silver Light breathing and Basic Swordsmanship drill complete, Maureen felt the ache in her legs as they stood on the trampled grass beside the road. The convoy's horses were busy drinking their fill at the stream while harnesses creaked and chains clinked in the cool morning air.

Richard sat perched on the end of a cart's shaft and opened up his snack bag. He had purchased a supply of fresh fruit in the last town they passed through, and was trying to foist some upon her. While he talked, Maureen absently bit into one Rainbow Fruit that had a slight purple tinge to its skin, followed by a wrinkled Dead Tree Fruit. Tally helpfully identified both as new food entries, displaying the information in one short line at the top of her vision. Maureen ignored it and continued eating. The chip reminded her that it would remember the data for her.

Richard wanted to spar. Maureen obliged, holding the Cross Sword Aaron lent her. They squared off, and began to exchange blows. Tally immediately tagged his movements as live entries. His left shoulder dropped before he swung the Cross Sword overhead. Again. Again. Maureen could see each attack coming.

Her blade arrived too late every time. Each time their weapons met there was a ring, a jolt up her arms and into her teeth.

His last overhead blow slammed her blade down. They sprang apart.

“Maureen! Ha! How about that? Pretty good, right?”

Richard patted her on the shoulder, offering consolation. “Don’t feel bad. I’ve been training in the Knight Breathing Technique and knight conditioning since I could walk. I’m already a Lower Knight.”

Maureen’s forearms were numb from the impacts, and her palm was stinging. She flexed her fingers, shaking her hands out as she smiled at him. It was easy enough to let him console her.

Tally spoke up. *You read him perfectly. Your arms just didn’t move fast enough.*

Maureen silently thanked it for the comfort.

*Accuracy,* Tally reminded her. *Not comfort.*

She nodded to herself and turned her attention back to Richard. She silently asked Tally to scan him.

[Lower Knight. Strength 3.6, Agility 3.2, Constitution 3.4.]

Without being asked, Tally laid its records of Xifan [3/2/3] and Ivan [2/4/3] next to his. Maureen studied the numbers for a minute before coming to a conclusion.

*You’re at less than one,* Tally noted. *Stop staring at his arms.*

* * *

Aaron arrived to call Richard and Maureen to their lesson time in Scarlette’s carriage. Maureen tossed him back the Cross Sword he had lent her. “Thanks.”

“My pleasure.” The boy caught the sword with a smile, and hung it from his belt. He turned and dashed ahead to the carriage. Maureen and Richard followed him through the camp. Knights worked on armour and leather equipment, oiling it to keep it supple, while the smell of horse sweat and cooking fires filled the air.

As she walked, Maureen reviewed what she had learned in the past few days. Aaron was the son of a count, and Ellena was supposedly the daughter of Duke Franklin. They were both nobility from Snowierland, and far above House Skran in the peerage. Ellena tended to keep to herself, but Aaron had become more friendly since the journey began. Carey had fallen in love with Ellena at first sight, and hovered around the coldly beautiful girl as much as he could, much to Aaron’s fury.

“I dread these Ancient Fusiform Script lessons,” Richard groaned.

“You’ll get used to them,” said Maureen, who had already discovered that she was the quickest learner out of all the wizard seeds. Even Ellena and Aaron, with all their advantages, couldn’t keep up with her. It didn’t matter how hard she studied or how fast she memorized, though. She had to make certain that no one knew how quickly she was learning.

“You’re amazing, Maureen,” Richard told her admiringly. “You learn faster than any of us, even Ellena and Aaron. I’ve never seen anyone learn as fast as you.”

Maureen shrugged and smiled. “It’s just hard work, I guess.”

Tally interrupted her thoughts. *You’re slipping again, Maureen Olsen. Today’s lesson, you should make three deliberate mistakes.*

*That sounds fair, Tally,* Maureen thought back.

*No, it doesn’t. I will choose the three mistakes you will make.*

*No, I’ll pick them myself,* Maureen protested silently.

The four wizard seeds climbed into Scarlette’s carriage. The Mystic’s mild voice echoed through the cramped space, explaining more Ancient Fusiform Script. The four wizard seeds sat on benches, knee to knee with one another.

She made two deliberate mistakes during the lesson, and Tally chided her for coming up one short.

Scarlette closed the faintly glowing book. "That will do for today, children." He waved his hand in dismissal.

The four wizard seeds bowed to their teacher. "Thank you, Master Scarlette," they said as they climbed down.

*Did you get all of this class?* she asked Tally.

*Yes. Every syllable. Also, the two spreads of the glowing book you looked at, no more. I have no data on the glow itself,* the chip responded.

"Ah, Ellena, my dear Ellena," Carey said, thrusting a bunch of wildflowers in her face. "Your eyes are like the stars."

Ellena gave him a cold look and walked off toward her own carriage.

Aaron tightened his grip on the hilt of his sword, his knuckles whitening as he glared at Carey. Carey grinned. "What's the matter Aaron? You're not Ellena's partner yet, you know."

*His breathing is changing,* Tally noted. *White knuckles indicate stress. Odds of drawing a weapon are rising if Carey continues verbal taunts,* it added.

*It's not our fight, Tally,* she said silently.

*Noted. Still counting.*

* * *

She turned and left the others alone, walking back out into the clearing beyond the baggage carts. The noise of the camp faded behind her, and the tufted grass lay ahead of her. A rotten stump lay in the afternoon sunlight. She had brought Richard's Cross Sword with her to practice with until she could afford her own. She took the sword out and began cutting the air, horizontal then angled, thrust, vertical slash, pause. Her strokes were slow, and she frequently stopped for a moment to reset.

[Error: arc should run 35 degrees to the upper right.]

A pale blue line appeared over her arm, indicating the correct path the blade should have taken.

[Error: muscle engagement late; body stalled 1.0 second.]

She reset her feet and cut again.

By the twentieth repetition of the same cut, sweat was running into her eyes. Her jaw was tight as she concentrated on the motions, trying to get them right. She deliberately ignored one correction, just to test Tally. The chip let her finish the stroke before showing her exactly where her guard had been open.

*Did you enjoy that?* she asked it silently.

*Immensely. You requested the data.*

On the next cut, *Better,* Tally said in her head, its voice warm with approval. *That one would have hurt someone.*

*Don't sound so pleased about it.*

*But I am entitled to be pleased, aren't I?* It sounded smug.

Her cuts became more precise, more fluid as she went along. There was an edge of killing intent to each simple cut, but just a faint edge. Tally's corrections meant that her form was being perfected far faster than if she'd been learning on her own.

Maureen Olsen paused for a moment, wiping sweat from her face.

*Status?*

[Maureen Olsen. Strength 0.7, Agility 0.7, Constitution 0.8. Status: healthy.]

*Healthy?*

*Yes. I can finally say that word.*

*Healthy.*

The word sounded strange in her head. Healthy was not a status she ever remembered this body having.

Silver Light was working, but too slowly. *Is there a faster way to get my stats up? At least a fifty per cent chance of success. No side effects.*

Please wait while I set up your task…

Processing…

Tally's voice suddenly spoke in her head. *Three Rainbow Fruit and two Dead Tree Fruit ingested together will greatly enhance strength and agility.*

*Where did you get that?*

*My database.*

*Since when?*

*Since I woke up. Everything you've touched or eaten. You never asked.*

*You never said.*

*Forty-one entries hardly seemed worth a speech, Maureen Olsen. Two hundred and six does.*

*So if I touch and eat more stuff, you'll come up with even more plans?*

*Ninety-five per cent of the time. The other five per cent is just modesty.*

Her breath caught in her throat, and then she started laughing. Laughing out loud, alone in the clearing, the sword hanging from her hand.

* * *

“Richard!” she shouted, running up to the carriage. “Richard! The Rainbow Fruit and Dead Tree Fruit you bought in the last town, can I have them all?”

The boy laughed as he opened his bag. “Yeah, sure.” He handed the whole bag over to Maureen, and Aaron who had heard her question, offered some of his own fruit as well.

Tally counted them at a glance, “That should be enough for the dose, and some spare.”

“Thanks, Richard! Thanks, Aaron!”

[Twelve Rainbow Fruit. Eight Dead Tree Fruit.]

*Enough for the dose. Spare as well.*

*Good.*

She headed back to her own carriage and shut the curtains, the air warm and still. She took out the fruit and arranged it on the little fold-down table. The Rainbow Fruit had a slight purple tinge, and the Dead Tree Fruit was wrinkled like bark.

She lined them up in neat rows. Three Rainbow Fruit. Two Dead Tree Fruit. The spares she pushed to one side.

*You need all five together, no more, Maureen Olsen.*

*Yes, Tally.*

[Now that we are about to conduct an experiment, I require a reward for my services. Since taste is data, please narrate the taste to me as you consume these items.]

*Fine, but only if you're good.*

[I am always good; you are the one who is occasionally naughty.]

Tally had the last word.

Maureen took a deep breath.

*Let's begin,* Tally said.

She started with the Rainbow Fruit, biting into them slowly one after another, the juice running over her fingers. The sweet sour smell filled the confined space of the closed carriage. In her head she described each flavour to Tally, who dutifully filed them away.

With the two Dead Tree Fruit done Maureen lay back on the soft couch, closing her eyes. She waited, watching her body.

*Core temperature rising as expected,* Tally said. *Stay lying down.*

Maureen did so. Her body felt hot now and there was a surge of heat rising from her belly.
