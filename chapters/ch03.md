It seemed to take forever. Then she saw the glow beneath her hand, a misty grey.

Her body felt like it had been scraped inside, all the stuffing knocked out of her. Something had come through her, touched her, then gone.

Tally flagged the dip in her pulse and respiration as she held her breath for a moment, then logged the contact as an event. The chip had no data on what the misty grey meant.

"Congratulations, future wizard," Scarlette said mildly. Maureen managed a weak smile then walked over to stand beside Carey, Ellena and Aaron. Ellena stood a hand's width away from Carey. Aaron appeared to be looking over the heads of the servants. She let her breath out in a long sigh, feeling herself grow steadier.

The Duke had Richard tested last, just in case. He had aptitude too. Richard grabbed her sleeve. "Maureen! We're wizards. I'm going to be a wizard!"

*Richard's heart rate is double yours. Perhaps one of you should sit down before he keels over.* Tally suggested helpfully.

The Duke laughed, the first time Maureen had ever heard him laugh aloud without restraint. "Yes, please take care of Richard from now on Mystic." Then he had servants show Scarlette and his party to their rooms.

* * *

Maureen and Carey sat down at the table. As servants they were used to waiting until everyone else had eaten before they could eat their fill. Often, they didn't get to eat anything at all.

Maureen cracked open the skin on the roast fowl with her teeth, feeling the grease slick between her fingers. Carey was using both hands to tear and chew meat.

*This body has never had a full plate of food in its records,* Tally noted. *The stomach is starved. Small bites and slow chewing are recommended.*

The Duke returned after a few moments. "Carey, Maureen, I am going to ask that you live here in the residence from this point forward."

"Yes Duke," Carey said.

"I appreciate the offer," Maureen replied politely. "However I have a grandfather to bury before I can move forward with my life."

The Duke looked up at her in surprise, then smiled. "Very well, as you wish."

At the Duke's nod the steward moved forward and placed a heavy purse next to her plate. "To settle your affairs and provide for your needs. With your back wages included."

Tally immediately began to count the contents of the purse by weight and sound. The sound of coins clinking against each other indicated that there was a good amount of gold within. At least one hundred gold, more likely closer to two hundred. It would be able to give her an exact count when she allowed it access to the coins. The chip also noticed the Duke's eyes travel from the purse to her face. He intended it to be remembered.

"Xifan, Ivan," The Duke called out, and the knights entered the hall. They knelt on one knee before their lord. "Please escort Mistress Olsen back to her home, and make certain no harm comes to her." His voice deepened. "Understood?"

Both knights briefly glanced at her before pounding their breastplates with their fists in assent. The sound rang up into the high, draughty windows.

* * *

The knights walked beside her, one to each shoulder. The sheer bulk of their bodies seemed to press against her like the heat from an open forge.

*Tally, examine them.* She thought silently.

[Xifan. Male. Strength 3, Agility 2, Constitution 3.]

[Ivan. Male. Strength 2, Agility 4, Constitution 3.]

A healthy human adult was given a base value of 1. Xifan had six times her strength, while Ivan had nearly six times her agility. Tally helpfully added. It also noted that since they entered the hall Xifan hadn't spoken even once, while Ivan was currently humming a tune.

"Well, I certainly feel a weight on me just standing next to you two," Maureen said half in jest.

"Of course, Lady Maureen." Ivan grinned, his brown hair flopping over his eyes as he nodded. "We've been training as knights since childhood. Using the Knight Breathing Technique."

*Knight Breathing Technique?* Tally repeated. *No data on that technique available. If a technique manual is made available all relevant data will be analysed.*

In other words, if she could get her hands on a book about the Knight's Breathing Technique, then Tally would analyse it for her. The chip sounded almost hungry at the prospect of new data. Maureen considered the name for a moment. Her fear had turned to an appetite to learn more.

By the time they reached the edge of the commoners' quarter she'd decided what she wanted to try first. Maureen stopped by a mason's yard, the sight of the knights ensuring prompt service. She paid for a simple, unadorned slab from her newly acquired purse.

The mason's chisel tick, tick, ticked against the stone as she waited. A fine grey dust settled on her sleeve, the letters taking shape. JOHN OLSEN. Xifan silently lifted the slab and slung it over his shoulder without being asked. They continued down into the slum.

* * *

"Little Maureen, I got Old John his coffin," Andrew called out, "he's sleeping sound in it now."

Then he saw the knights. His voice died along with the joke.

Tally flagged the break in his tone, mid-word, and the motion of his sooty hand rising to adjust his cap.

"Uncle Andrew, these are my guards," Maureen said firmly, "there is no need to worry about them."

"Little Maureen, you..." Andrew began, then stammered to a halt.

Maureen smiled at him. "Take me to Grandpa." The slum had no formal cemetery, just holes dug amongst the weeds at the edge of the waste ground that marked the boundary of the slum. Crows rose with a clatter of wings as they approached, disturbed from their perches on the smoking rubbish heaps. The wet clay showed through between the weeds and brambles. Old graves were just holes in the ground that had been filled in. Many of Old John's acquaintances had come despite there being nowhere to sit, and no service. Aunt Mary began berating Andrew for being late, but then she caught sight of the knights and fell silent. The crowd murmured once before falling silent, all eyes on the knights following Maureen's progress.

Tally was reading the crowd and reported four people edging away from the knights, and Mary, who was holding her breath, her hands twisted in her darned shawl.

Her knuckles were cracked and red from the lye she used to wash clothes. She pointed at the coffin without saying a word.

Old John was lying in his box, dressed in clean and neat clothing. His face was peaceful and kind. Maureen looked down at him for a long time, before sighing and gently closing the lid over him with steady hands. The wood was thin planks of raw pine, unvarnished. Someone had combed out Old John's tangled grey hair so it lay flat against his skull. Maureen felt a tightness in her throat as she looked down at the coffin. *Think of the gold as a debt being repaid,* Tally advised, *not charity. Give them an amount large enough they can't argue you down.*

Maureen took the purse from her belt and pressed it into Andrew's hands. "Take this. Leave the slum and live well." Aunt Mary began protesting but Maureen continued, "It is yours by right. Thank you for looking after me."

Andrew glanced inside the purse, spotting the glitter of gold coins. There must have been over a hundred of them. He flinched away from the purse. Aunt Mary protested more vehemently. Maureen gripped their hands firmly until they accepted the purse, then quietly told Ivan that the knights would see Andrew and Mary settled in the wealthy district.

* * *

Maureen accepted the shovel from Andrew and began to dig up beside the coffin. Ivan approached her. "Lady Maureen, perhaps we can help you."

"Thank you. No."

The clay clung stubbornly to the shovel blade. It was wet with the recent rains and every time she dug it up and tossed it aside, her sweat ran into her eyes. The handle had been worn smooth with use and her blistered palms rubbed against it as she worked. Blisters formed, broke and formed again. Tally kept count of each load of dirt she dug up and threw aside. She'd asked. The chip's calm voice would tell her the number of loads, reminding her that her heart rate was too high to maintain this pace for long. She ignored Tally and kept digging. The chip didn't argue.

When the hole was deep enough, Maureen stepped back and pushed the coffin in. Then she started filling the grave. No one left, they all stayed to watch. The first load of earth struck the thin wood lid, creating a hollow sound.

Tally flagged a voice. One it had on record from the day of the bread. It was coming through the crowd. Maureen looked up to see Angrey pushing his way out of the onlookers.

"He's dead, ain't he?" Angrey yelled. "Dead old ghost. Couldn't have left you more than them few silver coins. Give us the rest."

The Duke's knights moved to block him. Xifan's fist smashed into Angrey's face. Blood and teeth sprayed from his mouth as he was sent flying across the ground. Ivan kicked at him as he began begging. Then both knights turned to her.

"Lady Maureen."

Angrey's mouth opened and closed like a fish.

Tally didn't offer any advice, just confirmation of what she already knew. *Same man. Same voice.*

Maureen looked down at him. "Angrey," she said softly. "Do you remember? You took my bread." Then she turned back to her shovel.

Angrey's cries for mercy were drowned by the scrape of the shovel against the dirt. The dull wet thuds continued after his cries had stopped.

Maureen Olsen dug.

Tally told her once that Angrey had stopped breathing. She continued packing the earth down on top of Old John.

She patted down the low mound of earth, pressing firmly with her small hands. *This is Old John's grave. It's also the grave of the old Maureen. The Maureen who died grieving him. She's gone now. Only I remain.*

Maureen bent down and lifted the JOHN OLSEN slab, placing it at the head of the grave. She stood there for a while, then nodded. "Let's go."

Maureen Olsen didn't spare a glance for Angrey's body as she left. One by one oil lamps flickered to life across Skran City as they climbed the hill to the manor. The slum's black smoke was behind them.

Tally spoke quietly. *The letters are straight,* it told her. *They are on record now, for good.*

*Thank you.*
